Contact

For help, bug reports or feedback, email support@sudeha.in. Please mention your phone model and the level you were playing if you are reporting a problem.

Common questions

How do I play? Swipe anywhere on the board. Berry moves the way you swipe and Mint moves the mirror image (left and right swap, up and down stay). Get both blobs onto their goals at the same time.

One blob won't move. A blob that bumps into a wall, a crate it cannot push or a closed door stays where it is while the other one moves. Use that to line them up.

What do the special tiles do? Buttons open the doors of the same number while a blob stands on them. Swirls switch Mint between mirroring and copying. Spikes bounce a blob back. Portals send a blob to the matching portal of the same pair.

I am stuck on a level. Undo and restart are free. The Hint booster shows the next swipe, Unlink lets one blob move alone, and Skip solves the level. Every level has a solution.

How does Duo Duel work? Place the phone between two players. Each player swipes on their own half; the first to solve the puzzle wins the round, and the first to three rounds wins the match.

I bought coins but did not receive them. Purchases are handled by Apple. Reopen the Shop with an internet connection; if the coins still do not appear, email us with the date of the purchase, or ask Apple for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does the game need internet or an account? No. It works offline and has no accounts or ads.

Will I lose my progress? Progress is saved on your device only. Uninstalling the game removes it.
