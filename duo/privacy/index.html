Duo – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026 · Developer: Sudesh Chhipa

This policy explains how the mobile game Duo (“the game”) handles information. In short: the game does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking SDKs, and it does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves a small amount of data only on your device: levels unlocked, stars earned, coins, booster counts, your sound setting and your daily-challenge streak. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its app data.

In-app purchases

This version of the game has no in-app purchases. Coins and boosters are earned by playing. If purchases are added in a future version, this policy will be updated first.

Network access

The game works fully offline and sends no data to the developer or to anyone else.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country).

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features that handle personal information (for example advertising), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the game: please email support@sudeha.in.
