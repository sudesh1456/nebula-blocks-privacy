DUO

One swipe. Two blobs. Think!

Berry and Mint do everything together. Every swipe moves them both, but Mint is a mirror: swipe left and Berry goes left while Mint goes right. Bring both blobs home at the same moment.

  • Free to play
  • No ads

  • No account needed

How to play

  1. Swipe once

    Berry moves the way you swipe. Mint moves the mirror image: left and right swap, up and down stay.

  2. Use the walls

    A blob that bumps a wall stays put while the other keeps going. That's how you line them up.

  3. Land together

    Get both blobs onto their goals at the same moment. Fewer swipes earn more stars.

What's inside

60 puzzle levels

Each level introduces one idea at a time. A solver checks every one, so none is impossible.

Duo Duel

Two players, one phone. Race on the same puzzle from opposite ends; first to three rounds wins.

Daily Puzzle

A new puzzle every day, with streaks and a coin bonus.

Endless mode

Fresh puzzles made on your phone, getting trickier as you go.

Swirls, spikes and portals

Buttons and doors, crates to push, swirl tiles that switch Mint between mirroring and copying.

No ads, no account

No ads and no personal data collected. Optional coin packs are sold through the App Store.
