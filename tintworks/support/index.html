Common questions

How do I play? Drag a glass block with your finger; it stays where you let go. Let go on a paint puddle to soak up its colour, then guide the block out through the doorway of exactly that colour.

How do colours mix? Red + blue = purple, yellow + blue = green, red + yellow = orange, and all three make brown. Rinse basins wash a block clear, and prisms flip a colour to its opposite.

How do I fuse blocks? In the Fusion chapter, push one block against another and hold; they melt into one bigger block with both colours.

I am stuck. Use Undo, Restart or the Hint button. Every puzzle is checked to be solvable.

What is the Daily Window? A new puzzle every day. Each one fills a pane of that month's window and keeps your streak going.

Does the game need internet or an account? No. It works offline and has no accounts, ads or purchases.

Will I lose my progress? Progress is saved on your device only. Uninstalling the game removes it.
