Tintworks: Color Mix Puzzle

Every block is a little glass vessel, and its colour depends on where you set it down. Drop it in paint, mix new colours, and guide it out through the doorway of exactly its colour. Every puzzle you solve adds a pane to a stained-glass window.

  • 120 puzzles in six chapters: Paint Pools, Mixing, Rinse, Fusion, Stained Glass and Prisms
  • Red + blue = purple, yellow + blue = green, red + yellow = orange
  • Rinse basins, prisms that flip colours, and blocks that fuse into one
  • A new Daily Window puzzle every day, with a streak to keep
  • Undo, restart and hints; no timers, no lives, no ads
  • Plays offline; no accounts and no data collected
