Sharpmind – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 October 2026 · Developer: Sudesh Chhipa

This policy explains how the app Sharpmind (“the app”), on Android and iOS, handles information. In short: the app collects nothing. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking SDKs.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for your name, email address or any other personal information, and it does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the app saves a small amount of data only on your device: your scores, best scores and level in each game, your ratings, which games of the daily workout you have played, your streak, simple usage counts (such as how many games you have played) and your sound setting. This data never leaves your device. You can erase it at any time with Settings > Reset progress; it is also deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its app data.

Network access

The app works fully offline and sends nothing over the network. The only time it opens a connection is when you tap Privacy policy in Settings, which opens this page in your browser.

Health

Sharpmind is a set of games for practice and entertainment. It is not a medical device, it does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition, and its scores are not a measure of intelligence or health.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features that handle personal information (for example ads, analytics or in-app purchases), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app: please email support@sudeha.in (mention Sharpmind).
