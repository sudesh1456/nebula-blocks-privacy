Squiggle Zoo – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 November 2026 · Developer: Sudesh Chhipa

This policy explains how the mobile game Squiggle Zoo (“the game”) handles information. In short: the game does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking SDKs. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves a small amount of data only on your device: which letters and numbers have been traced, stars, coins and your sound setting. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its app data.

Voice

The spoken lines are recordings that ship inside the game. If a recording is missing, the game may use the text-to-speech voice built into your phone. Nothing is recorded from you and nothing is sent anywhere.

Network access and purchases

The game works fully offline and sends no data to the developer or to anyone else. It has no in-app purchases.

Children

The game is made for young children and their parents. Because it collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country). It contains no ads and no links that lead out of the game.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features that handle personal information, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the game: please email support@sudeha.in.
