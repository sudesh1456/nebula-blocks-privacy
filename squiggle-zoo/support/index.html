Contact

For help, bug reports or feedback, email support@sudeha.in. Please mention your phone model and the letter or number you were on if you are reporting a problem.

Common questions

How do I play? Put your finger on the glowing dot and follow the road to trace the letter or number. When you finish, its critter wakes up. Then spell a word and count some objects to complete the level.

The tracing will not accept my finger. Start on the glowing dot and keep your finger on the road. If you slip off, lift and begin again from where the ink stopped.

I cannot hear anything. Check that the phone is not on silent and the volume is up, and that the sound button in the game is on. Tap the speaker buttons to hear a prompt again.

What is My Zoo? Every letter and number you trace becomes a critter in your zoo. Tap a friend to hear its name.

Does the game need internet or an account? No. It works offline and has no accounts, ads or purchases.

Will I lose my progress? Progress is saved on your device only. Uninstalling the game removes it.
