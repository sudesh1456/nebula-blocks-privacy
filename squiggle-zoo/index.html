Squiggle Zoo

Every letter and number is a sleepy critter. Trace it with your finger to wake it up, then spell a word and count some objects to finish the level.

  • Trace A to Z and 0 to 9 along a glowing road
  • Every letter, number and word is read aloud
  • Spell picture words by dragging letter tiles
  • Count objects by tapping them
  • Collect all 36 critters in My Zoo
  • No ads, no accounts, no data collected
