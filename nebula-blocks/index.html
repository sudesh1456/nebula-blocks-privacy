NEBULA BLOCKS

Slide. Sort. Launch.

Drag glowing blocks across a crowded board and send each one out through the gate of its own colour. Easy to learn, and the right order takes some thinking.

  • Free
  • No ads
  • Plays offline
  • No account needed

How to play

  1. Drag a block

    It glides cell by cell under your finger and stays wherever you let go.

  2. Find its gate

    Push the block into a gate of the same colour and it launches off the board.

  3. Clear the board

    Blocks get in each other's way, so the order matters. Fewer moves earn more stars.

What's inside

44 puzzle levels

From gentle warm-ups to real brain-benders. Every level is checked by a solver, so none is impossible.

Endless mode

Fresh puzzles made on your phone, one stage after another, getting harder as you go.

Daily Challenge

One new puzzle every day. Keep your streak going and collect bonus coins.

Asteroids and shields

Asteroids crumble as you clear blocks, and shielded gates open after enough launches.

Helpful boosters

Undo a move, ask for a hint, blast a block with a rocket, or freeze the clock.

Nothing to sign up for

No ads, no accounts, and no personal data collected. It works without internet.
