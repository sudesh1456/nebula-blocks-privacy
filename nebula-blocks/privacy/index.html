Nebula Blocks – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026 · Developer: Sudeha (partnership firm, Jaipur, India)

This policy explains how the mobile game Nebula Blocks (“the game”) handles information. In short: we (Sudeha) do not collect or store any personal information ourselves. From version 1.1 the game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device information to show and measure those ads, as described below.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts and no sign-in, and we run no servers that receive your data. The game does not access your contacts, precise location, camera, microphone or files.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Version 1.1 and later show ads provided by Google AdMob: optional “rewarded” ads you can choose to watch for a bonus (for example double coins or extra time), and an occasional full-screen ad between levels. To serve, limit and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device’s advertising ID (which you can reset or delete in your Android settings),
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • app interaction and diagnostic data, such as which ads were shown and whether they were tapped.

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us, under Google’s Privacy Policy. See also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can refuse. Everywhere, you can opt out of personalised ads by deleting or resetting your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings › Google › Ads / Privacy › Ads).

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves a small amount of data only on your device: levels unlocked, stars earned, coins, booster counts, your sound setting and your daily-challenge streak. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its app data.

Network access

Gameplay works fully offline. When the device is online, the game connects to Google’s ad servers to load ads.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country).

Changes to this policy

If the game’s data handling changes, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the game: please email support@sudeha.in.
