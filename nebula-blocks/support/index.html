Contact

For help, bug reports or feedback, email support@sudeha.in. Please mention your phone model and the level you were playing if you are reporting a problem.

Common questions

How do I play? Drag a block with your finger. Push it into a gate of the same colour that is wide enough and it leaves the board. Clear every block to finish the level.

A numbered grey block or a shielded gate is in my way. Asteroids crumble and shields open once you have cleared the number of blocks shown on them, so clear other blocks first.

I am stuck on a level. Use Undo to step back, or the Hint booster to see a move. Every level has a solution.

Does the game need internet or an account? No. It works offline and has no accounts, ads or purchases.

Will I lose my progress? Progress is saved on your device only. Uninstalling the game removes it.
