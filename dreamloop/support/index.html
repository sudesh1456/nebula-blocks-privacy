Common questions

How does it track my sleep? Wear your Apple Watch to bed. Dreamloop reads the sleep your watch records in Apple Health. If nothing was recorded, it detects your sleep from wrist movement and heart rate.

I see no data. Open the Health app > profile > Apps > Dreamloop and make sure sleep, heart rate and activity are allowed. Then pull down on the Sleep screen to refresh. Data appears after your first night.

Are the sleep stages accurate? Stages from Apple Watch come from Apple. Stages for nights Dreamloop detects itself are estimates from heart rate and movement.

What is the sleep score? A 0–100 number from time asleep against your goal, sleep efficiency, deep and REM share, and how regular your bedtime is.

Do I need an account or internet? No. There are no accounts and no ads, and your data is not uploaded.

The morning notification is late. iOS decides when apps may run in the background, so it can be delayed. Opening the app always refreshes your data.
