Dreamloop

Automatic sleep tracking for iPhone and Apple Watch

Wear your watch to bed and open Dreamloop in the morning. It shows how long you slept, how well, and how that compares with your goal, with no button to press and no account.

Features

  • Reads your sleep from Apple Health, or detects it from movement and heart rate
  • Sleep score, stage timeline, efficiency, heart rate and HRV
  • Sleep debt, 7- and 30-night trends, bedtime reminder and a morning summary
  • Apple Watch app
  • Private: your data stays on your devices
