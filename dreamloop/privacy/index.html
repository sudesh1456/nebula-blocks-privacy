Dreamloop – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026 · Developer: Sudesh Chhipa

This policy explains how Dreamloop (“the app”) for iPhone and Apple Watch handles information. In short: your sleep and health data stays on your devices and in Apple Health. We do not receive it.

Health data the app uses

With your permission, the app reads from Apple Health: sleep analysis, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, step count and active energy. It uses them only to show how you slept and, when your watch did not record sleep, to detect it from movement and heart rate. If you turn on Save detected sleep to Health, the app writes the detected sleep back to Apple Health. You can change or revoke access at any time in the Health app.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no servers, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking SDKs. Health data is processed on your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else, and is never used for advertising or sold.

Stored on your device

Settings (sleep goal, wake time, reminders) are saved on your device. Notifications, if you enable them, are scheduled locally on your device.

Medical disclaimer

Dreamloop is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition, and sleep stages and scores are estimates. Talk to a doctor if you are worried about your sleep.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version handles information differently, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
