Contact

For help, bug reports or feedback, email support@sudeha.in. Please mention your phone model and the trip you were on if you are reporting a problem.

Common questions

How do I play? Pick a trip on the Home screen. Bao asks a question out loud; tap the right picture, match the shadow or count the objects. Finish the trip to earn its stamp.

How do I open the next trips? Trips open one after another. Finish the trips in a passport to open the next passport.

I cannot hear anything. Check that the phone is not on silent and the volume is up, and that “Voice and sounds” is on in the Me tab.

What is in the Play tab? Memory Match, Bubble Pop and Sticker Scene. Stickers come from the trips you finish.

How do I start over? Open Me, then “For grown-ups”, answer the sum and choose Start Over.

Does the game need internet or an account? No. It works offline and has no accounts, ads or purchases.

Will I lose my progress? Progress is saved on your device only. Uninstalling the game removes it.
