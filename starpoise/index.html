Starpoise: Balance Puzzle

An eight-by-eight plate balances on a single star. Drag pieces onto it and fill rows and columns to clear them. Every cell pulls its side down, harder the further it sits from the centre, so each move is two questions: does it fit, and what does it do to the balance?

  • Three kinds of matter: gas weighs 1, rock 2, ember 3
  • A balance dial that shows where the plate leans and where your next piece would send it
  • Poise streaks: keep the bubble centred to multiply your line scores
  • Endless runs that get heavier, and a daily plate with the same pieces for everyone
  • No timers, no lives, no ads
  • Plays offline; no accounts and no data collected
