Common questions

How do I play? Drag a piece from the tray onto the plate. A full row or column clears. The run ends when none of your pieces can be placed.

Why was my move refused? Every cell pulls its side of the plate down, harder the further it is from the centre. If a move would lean the plate too far, the marker on the dial turns red and the piece goes back to the tray. Try a spot nearer the centre, or on the lighter side.

What do the dots on the cells mean? Their weight: gas has one dot and weighs 1, rock has two and weighs 2, ember has three and weighs 3.

What is a poise streak? Each move that ends with the bubble in the green centre of the dial adds to the streak. A streak multiplies the score of the lines you clear, up to five times.

What changes with the stage? Every five lines the stage goes up: heavier pieces are dealt and the plate tolerates less lean.

What is the daily plate? A run with the same pieces for everyone that day. Play it as often as you like; your best score of the day is kept.

Does the game need internet or an account? No. It works offline and has no accounts, ads or purchases.

Will I lose my scores? Scores are saved on your device only. Uninstalling the game removes them.
