Starpoise – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026 · Developer: Sudesh Chhipa

This policy explains how the game Starpoise ("the app") handles information. In short: the app collects nothing. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking SDKs.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for your name, email address or any other personal information, and it does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the app saves a small amount of data only on your device: your best scores, which daily plates you have played (for your streak) and your sound setting. This data never leaves your device. It is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Network access

The app works fully offline and sends nothing over the network. The daily plate is created on your device from the date.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features that handle personal information (for example ads, analytics or in-app purchases), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the game: please email support@sudeha.in (mention Starpoise).
